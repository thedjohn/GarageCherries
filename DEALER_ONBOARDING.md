# Welcome to GarageCherries — Dealer Setup Guide

This guide walks you through setting up your dealership account on GarageCherries, from applying to getting your first vehicles live on the site.

---

## 1. Apply

Submit your dealership application at **[garagecherries.com/dealer/apply](https://www.garagecherries.com/dealer/apply)**. You'll need:

- Your name, email, and phone number
- Dealership name, city, and state (street address and ZIP are optional but recommended)
- Your website (optional)
- Specialties (e.g. "Classic Cars, Muscle Cars, Trucks")
- A short description of your dealership — years in business, what you specialize in, approximate inventory size

Our team reviews every application before approving it.

## 2. Set Your Password

Once approved, you'll receive an email with a link to set up your password. Click it and follow the prompts — this creates your login.

> **Note:** that link is single-use and time-limited. If it's expired by the time you click it, just go to **[garagecherries.com/dealer/login](https://www.garagecherries.com/dealer/login)** and use "Forgot password?" to get a new one.

## 3. Sign In

Sign in at **[garagecherries.com/dealer/login](https://www.garagecherries.com/dealer/login)**. This takes you to your Dealer Dashboard, which has five tabs:

| Tab | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **Overview** | Snapshot of your active listings, views, and watchers |
| **Inventory** | Add, edit, renew, or mark vehicles sold |
| **Inquiries** | Messages from interested buyers |
| **Offers** | Offers buyers have submitted on your vehicles |
| **Settings** | Your dealer profile, locations, and inventory feed setup |

## 4. Complete Your Dealer Profile

Go to **Settings**. This information appears on your public dealer page and every listing you post, so it's worth filling out fully:

- **Logo** — JPG, PNG, or WebP, under 2 MB. Click "Upload Logo."
- **Dealership Name, Phone, Address, City, State, ZIP**
- **Website**
- **Specialties** (comma-separated)
- **About / Description** — what buyers see on your dealer page

Click **Save Changes** when done. (Your login email is fixed and can't be changed here — contact us if it needs to change.)

## 5. Add Locations (Multi-Location Dealers Only)

If you sell from more than one physical location, add each one under the **Locations** section in Settings — city/state, address, phone, and email per location. Mark one as **Primary**; that's the one shown as your main contact info everywhere else on the site. If you only have one location, skip this — your dealer profile above already covers it.

## 6. Add Your Inventory

You have two options, and you can use either or both at once.

### Option A — Add Vehicles Manually

From the **Inventory** tab, click **+ Add Vehicle**. Required fields: Year, Make, Model, Condition, Body Style, Fuel Type, Transmission, Drive Type, Price, Description, and at least one photo. Mileage, City, and State are optional (city/state pre-fill from your dealer profile). VIN is optional but recommended.

Vehicles you add manually go live immediately — there's no waiting period.

### Option B — Automatic Inventory Feed Sync

If your inventory management system can export a data feed, connect it once under **Settings → Feed Sync** and your listings stay in sync automatically (once a day, at an hour you choose), instead of manual data entry. There's also a **Sync now** button for an immediate one-off sync anytime.

Three ways to connect, depending on what your inventory system supports:

- **Direct URL (HTTPS)** — if your system publishes a hosted feed URL, just paste it in.
- **SFTP (your server)** — if you host your own SFTP server, give us the host, port, username, password, and remote file path, and we'll pull the file from you.
- **SFTP — we host** — if your export tool can only *push* to an SFTP destination (rather than publish a URL or let us pull), click **"Generate Credentials"** and we'll create one for you. You'll be shown a host, port, username, and password **once** — save it immediately, it can't be retrieved again later (only regenerated). Point your inventory system's export at that server, and **always upload the file under the exact name `inventory.csv`** — each new upload replaces the last one.

Not sure which applies to your setup? Contact us and we'll help you figure out the right option — and if your export format isn't one we've already configured for, let us know before your first sync so we can set it up correctly on our end.

## 7. What Happens Next

- New vehicles (however they're added) appear on the site right away.
- Listings run for 30 days; you'll get a reminder before yours expire so you can renew.
- Each new listing is automatically posted to the GarageCherries Facebook Page and cross-posted as a short video to Instagram and YouTube.
- Buyer messages show up in your **Inquiries** tab, and any offers submitted on your vehicles show up in **Offers** — respond to both from the dashboard.
- When a vehicle sells, mark it **Sold** from the Inventory tab so it comes off active listings.
